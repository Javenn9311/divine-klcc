Why buyers shortlist it

An address in the heart of KL, built for global living

400m to KLCC

Covered walkway to the Jalan Ampang–KLCC junction, with MRT Persiaran KLCC and LRT KLCC within walking distance.

The Pulse sky club

Infinity lap pool, hydromassage pool, heated jacuzzi and floating lounge on Level 79, with the gymnasium on Level 80, all facing the Twin Towers.

Dual-key types

Type B to B5 split into two keys, with a sub-meter for the second unit: stay in one, rent out the other.

Made for short stays

Smart kiosk check-in and check-out, plus card or QR guest access to their floor only.

Award-winning designers

Architecture by Konzepte, interiors by Ministry of Design, landscape by SD2.

Smart, fitted homes

Signature kitchen, Panasonic air-conditioning, smart curtains, lighting scenes and smart TV. GreenRE Gold provision.

The design team

Crafted by award-winning designers

Three studios shaped Divine KLCC, from the tower to the interiors to the sky gardens, with every unit furnished and finished by Signature.

Architecture

Konzepte

Architect: Nicholas Ling

A team across three countries. Designed the tower to glow at dusk, with signature fins that follow the sun’s path through the day.

Known for
  • Pavilion and Ceylon Hill, Kuala Lumpur
  • Arrow on Swanston, Melbourne
  • The Commune Sky Lofts, Phnom Penh
Interiors

Ministry of Design

Founder & Director of Design: Colin Seah

185 global accolades. A cinematic arrival through a tunnel-like entrance, sculptural lounges, lobby dining and a three-storey destination for food and drink.

Known for
  • The Standard, Singapore
  • Citi Wealth Hub, Singapore
Landscape

SD2

Four landscape decks layered like a rainforest, from a shaded forest floor up to a sky-level retreat, each planned around how much light it receives.

Known for
